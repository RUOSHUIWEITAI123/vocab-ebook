## Part 1 — The Campus

On his first day at the **campus**（大学校园）, Zhang Wei felt a mix of excitement and **anxiety**. The **academic**（学术的） atmosphere was **extraordinary**（非凡的，出色的）— unlike anything he had experienced in high school. As a freshman, he knew he needed to **acquire**（取得，获得） new skills and **adapt**（适应） quickly to university life.

大学校园第一天，张伟感受到兴奋与焦虑交织。学术氛围非凡——与高中截然不同。作为新生，他知道必须快速获得新技能并适应大学生活。

He checked the **calendar**（日历，日程表） on his phone. His first class was **compulsory**（强制性的，义务的） English, followed by an **optional**（可选的） seminar on **innovation**（创新）. The university **catalog**（目录） listed hundreds of courses, but he had to **calculate**（计算） his **budget**（预算） carefully. As a **liberal**（自由的，开明的） arts student, he could **range**（范围） across many **subjects**（主题，学科）.

他查看了手机上的日历。第一节是必修英语课，然后是一个关于创新的可选研讨会。大学目录列出了数百门课程，但他必须仔细计算预算。作为一个学文科的学生，他可以跨很多学科范围选课。

Walking across the **campus**, he noticed a **band**（乐队） playing near the **cafeteria**. The music was **terrific**（极好的）. Students were **wandering**（漫步） between buildings, some looking **casual**（随便的） in shorts and flip-flops. He spotted a **vacant**（空着的） bench and sat down to read the **application**（申请） form for a student **organization**（组织）.

穿过校园时，他注意到食堂附近有一支乐队在演奏。音乐棒极了。学生们在建筑之间漫步，有些人穿着随便的短裤和人字拖。他发现一张空着的长椅，坐下来阅读学生组织的申请表。

His roommate, Li Tao, was a **sophomore** who seemed to know everything about the school. "You need to **identify**（识别，鉴定） your goals early," Li Tao advised. "The **competition** here is **intense**（激烈的）. Don't be **idle**（懒散的） — every hour counts." He spoke with the **confidence** of someone who had already figured out the system.

他的室友李涛似乎对这所学校了如指掌。"你需要早点识别你的目标，"李涛建议道。"这里的竞争很激烈。不要懒散——每一小时都很重要。"他说话带着一种已经摸透体制的人的自信。

## Part 2 — The Professor

Professor Wang was a **distinguished**（杰出的） scholar with an **intellectual**（智力的，知识分子） presence that commanded respect. His **lecture** on **urban**（城市的） development was the most popular on **campus**. "To understand a city," he said, "you must examine its **infrastructure** — the **water** supply, the **transportation**（交通） network, the **domestic**（国内的，家庭的） economy."

王教授是一位杰出的学者，有着令人尊敬的知识分子气质。他关于城市发展的讲座在校园里最受欢迎。"要理解一座城市，"他说，"你必须审视它的基础设施——供水、交通网络、国内经济。"

Zhang Wei was deeply **impressed**（印象深刻的）. He wanted to **inquire**（询问，打听） about the professor's research but felt **shy**. Finally, he gathered the courage to ask about the **impact**（影响） of **rural**（农村的） migration on **suburban**（郊区的） communities. The professor smiled. "That's an **excellent** question. See me after class."

张伟印象深刻。他想询问教授的研究但又感到害羞。最后，他鼓起勇气问了关于农村人口迁移对郊区社区的影响。教授微笑着说："这是个很好的问题。课后找我。"

In their conversation, Professor Wang spoke about how **individual**（个人的） choices **inevitably**（不可避免地） affect the **collective**. "**Nowadays**（如今）, young people face **unprecedented**（前所未有的） challenges," he said. "You must learn to **distinguish** between what's **urgent** and what's **important**. Don't let **trivial**（琐碎的） matters **consume**（消耗） your energy."

在交谈中，王教授谈到个人选择如何不可避免地影响集体。"如今，年轻人面临着前所未有的挑战，"他说。"你必须学会区分紧急和重要。不要让琐碎的事情消耗你的精力。"

Professor Wang also warned about the **danger** of information **overload**. "The **internet**（互联网） gives us access to endless data, but **merely**（仅仅） having information is not enough. You must **cultivate** the ability to **analyze** and **synthesize**. That's what **education** truly means."

王教授还警告了信息过载的危险。"互联网给我们提供了无尽的数据，但仅仅有信息是不够的。你必须培养分析和综合的能力。这才是教育的真正意义。"

## Part 3 — The Library

Zhang Wei spent most evenings in the library, determined to **acquire** knowledge and **establish**（建立） a solid foundation. The librarian, an **elderly** woman with an **extraordinary**（非凡的） memory, could **locate** any book instantly. She recommended an **obscure**（晦涩的，默默无闻的） text on **urban** planning that had been **overlooked** for decades.

张伟大多数晚上都在图书馆里，决心获取知识并建立坚实的基础。图书馆员是一位有着非凡记忆力的老妇人，能立刻找出任何书。她推荐了一本关于城市规划的晦涩著作，这本书几十年来一直被忽视。

"The **material**（材料，物质） in this book is **dense** but **valuable**," she said. "Don't let its age **fool** you." Zhang Wei opened the **volume** to find detailed **accounts** of how cities had **evolved** over centuries. The book contained **evidence**（证据） that early **urban** planners had **anticipated** many modern problems.

"这本书的材料很密集但很有价值，"她说。"不要被它的年代所欺骗。"张伟打开这本书，发现详细记载了几个世纪以来城市是如何演变的。书中包含证据表明早期城市规划者已经预见了许多现代问题。

As he read, he came across a **passage** about **sustainable**（可持续的） development. The author argued that **innovation**（创新） must be **combined** with **tradition** to create truly **resilient** cities. Zhang Wei began to take **notes**, hoping to **utilize**（利用） this knowledge in his own research. He felt a sense of **purpose** that he had never experienced before.

阅读中，他读到一段关于可持续发展的文章。作者认为创新必须与传统相结合才能创造真正有韧性的城市。张伟开始做笔记，希望在自己的研究中利用这些知识。他感到一种前所未有的目标感。

Outside the library window, the **campus** was quiet. The **mild**（温和的） autumn breeze rustled the leaves. Zhang Wei realized that moments like this — **peaceful**, **focused**, **meaningful** — were what made university life **precious**. He made a **mental**（精神的，心理的） note to **appreciate**（欣赏，感激） these quiet hours.

图书馆窗外，校园很安静。温和的秋风拂动着树叶。张伟意识到这样的时刻——平静、专注、有意义——正是大学生活珍贵的原因。他在心里默默记录，要学会欣赏这些宁静的时光。

## Part 4 — The Conflict

Not everything at university went smoothly. Zhang Wei accidentally **offend**（冒犯，触犯） his roommate by **borrowing** his laptop without permission. The **offence**（冒犯，过错） created **tension** between them. "You should have **inquired**（询问） first," Li Tao said, his voice **strained**（紧张的，绷紧的）.

不是所有大学的事都顺利。张伟因为未经允许借用室友的笔记本电脑而冒犯了他。这个过错在他们之间制造了紧张气氛。"你应该先问一下，"李涛说，他的声音很紧张。

Zhang Wei felt a deep **sense**（感觉，意识） of regret. He had been **inconsiderate** and knew he needed to **apologize**. "I **acknowledge**（承认） my mistake," he said. "It was an **invasion**（侵犯，入侵） of your **privacy**（隐私）. I'll make it right." He offered to **compensate** for any trouble he had caused.

张伟感到深深的内疚。他考虑不周，知道必须道歉。"我承认我的错误，"他说。"这是对你隐私的侵犯。我会弥补的。"他提出要补偿自己造成的任何麻烦。

The **conflict**（冲突） taught Zhang Wei an important lesson about **boundaries**（界限）. Living in **close** quarters required **mutual** respect. The **incident** also made him reflect on the **nature** of friendship. "A true friend doesn't just **ignore**（忽视） your faults," Li Tao said later, "but helps you **recognize**（认识到） and **correct** them."

这次冲突给张伟上了关于界限的重要一课。在近距离生活需要相互尊重。这个事件也让他反思友谊的本质。"真正的朋友不只是忽视你的缺点，"李涛后来说，"而是帮你认识到并改正它们。"

Their relationship slowly **recovered**. Zhang Wei became more **sensitive**（敏感的，体贴的） to others' feelings. He learned that **communication** was key — without it, even **minor** issues could **escalate** into major **problems**（问题）. This experience made him **appreciate**（感激） the **value** of **honesty** and **forgiveness**.

他们的关系慢慢恢复了。张伟对他人感受变得更加敏感。他意识到沟通是关键——没有沟通，即使是小问题也可能升级为大问题。这段经历让他感激诚实和宽恕的价值。

## Part 5 — The Competition

The university announced a **prestigious** scholarship **competition**. Students were required to **submit** a **proposal**（提议） addressing an **urban** problem. Zhang Wei's heart **raced** with **excitement** — this was his chance to **apply**（应用） everything he had learned.

大学宣布了一场有声望的奖学金竞赛。学生需要提交一份解决城市问题的提案。张伟心情激动——这是他应用所学一切的机会。

He chose to **address** the issue of **rural**（农村的） poverty and its **impact** on **urban** migration. His **project**（项目） would **examine** how **investing** in **rural** **infrastructure** could **reduce** the **pressure** on cities. "This is an **ambitious** topic," Professor Wang said. "You'll need **compelling** **evidence**（证据）."

他选择研究农村贫困及其对城市迁移的影响。他的项目将研究如何通过投资农村基础设施来减少对城市的压力。"这是个有野心的题目，"王教授说。"你需要有说服力的证据。"

Zhang Wei spent weeks gathering **data**, conducting **interviews**, and **analyzing** **statistics**. The **process** was **tedious**（冗长的，乏味的） at times, but he remained **determined**. He learned to **utilize**（利用） various **software** tools and **consult** with **experts** in the field. His **proficiency**（熟练） with data analysis grew rapidly.

张伟花了几周时间收集数据、进行访谈、分析统计数据。这个过程有时很乏味，但他保持着决心。他学会利用各种软件工具并向领域专家咨询。他数据分析的熟练度迅速增长。

The night before the **deadline**, he felt a mixture of **confidence** and **doubt**. Had he done enough? Was his **argument** **convincing**? He recalled something his professor had said: "The **pursuit** of **excellence** is never **comfortable**. **Embrace** the **discomfort** — it means you're growing."

截止日期前一晚，他感到既自信又怀疑。他做得够吗？他的论证有说服力吗？他想起教授说过的话："追求卓越从来不会舒服。拥抱不适——这意味着你在成长。"

## Part 6 — The Victory

A week later, the results were **announced**. Zhang Wei had won second place! The **recognition** filled him with **immense**（巨大的） joy. His friends **organized** a small **celebration**. "Your hard work **paid off**," Li Tao said. "You've **earned** this."

一周后，结果公布了。张伟获得了第二名！这种认可让他充满了巨大的喜悦。朋友们组织了一个小小的庆祝会。"你的努力得到了回报，"李涛说。"这是你应得的。"

The prize included a **grant**（拨款，补助金） to continue his research and an **opportunity** to **present** at a **national** conference. Zhang Wei felt like he had crossed an important **threshold**. His **achievement**（成就） proved that **persistence** could **overcome** **obstacles**.

奖品包括一笔继续研究的拨款，以及在全国会议上展示的机会。张伟感觉自己跨过了一个重要的门槛。他的成就证明了坚持可以克服障碍。

At the conference, he met students from other universities — some from **elite** institutions, others from **remote**（偏远的） schools. What **united** them was a shared **passion** for knowledge. Zhang Wei delivered his **presentation** with **poise** and **clarity**, earning **applause** from the **audience**.

在会议上，他遇到了来自其他大学的学生——有些来自精英学府，有些来自偏远学校。团结他们的是对知识的共同热情。张伟镇定而清晰地进行展示，赢得了观众的掌声。

A professor from another university approached him afterward. "Your research is **impressive**（印象深刻的）," she said. "I especially like how you **integrated**（整合） **economic** and **social** factors." She offered him a **summer** internship at her **institute**. Zhang Wei realized that this **opportunity** could **alter**（改变） the entire **trajectory** of his career.

之后，一位来自其他大学的教授走过来。"你的研究令人印象深刻，"她说。"我特别喜欢你是如何整合经济和社会因素的。"她给他在她的研究所提供了一个暑期实习机会。张伟意识到这个机会可能改变他职业生涯的整个轨迹。

## Part 7 — The Shadow

Despite his success, Zhang Wei faced inner **struggles**. The **pressure** to **sustain**（维持） his performance was **intense**. He began to experience **frequent**（频繁的） headaches and **occasional** bouts of **insomnia**. His **physical**（身体的） health was declining under the **strain**（压力，拉力）.

尽管成功了，张伟仍然面临内心的挣扎。维持表现的压力很激烈。他开始频繁头痛，偶尔失眠。在压力下，他的身体健康在下降。

"Your **body**（身体） is sending you a **warning**," the campus doctor said. "If you don't **reduce** your stress, the **consequences**（后果） could be **serious**. You need to **establish** a healthier **routine**." The doctor prescribed rest, **exercise**, and a **balanced** diet.

"你的身体在向你发出警告，"校医说。"如果你不减轻压力，后果可能很严重。你需要建立一个更健康的日常。"医生开了休息、运动和均衡饮食的处方。

Zhang Wei realized he had been **neglecting** his well-being. He had **fooled** himself into thinking that **sacrifice** was **noble**. But what **value** did **achievement** have if it came at the **cost** of health? He began to **reflect** on his **priorities**.

张伟意识到他一直在忽视自己的健康。他欺骗自己，认为牺牲是高尚的。但如果成就以健康为代价，那它还有什么价值呢？他开始反思自己的优先级。

He started going for **regular** runs around the **campus** **track**（跑道）. The **physical** activity cleared his mind. He also **joined** a **meditation** group, where he learned to **calm** his thoughts. The **practice** helped him **cope** with academic **demands** without feeling **overwhelmed**.

他开始定期在校园跑道上跑步。身体活动让他头脑清醒。他还加入了一个冥想小组，在那里学会了平静思绪。这种练习帮助他应对学业需求而不感到不堪重负。

"**Balance** is not a **destination** but a **practice**," his meditation teacher said. "Every day, you must **choose** what matters most and let go of the rest." These words **resonated** deeply with Zhang Wei.

"平衡不是目的地，而是一种练习，"他的冥想老师说。"每天，你必须选择最重要的事，然后放手其他。"这些话深深地引起了张伟的共鸣。

## Part 8 — The Crisis

Winter brought a new challenge: Li Tao's father fell **seriously** ill. The **news** was a **terrible**（可怕的） shock. Li Tao's family faced **enormous** medical **bills**, and he **struggled** to **concentrate** on his studies. His **sorrow**（悲伤） was **visible**（明显的） to everyone.

冬天带来了新的挑战：李涛的父亲病重。这个消息带来了可怕的打击。李涛的家庭面临着巨大的医疗账单，他难以集中精力学习。他的悲伤在每个人看来都很明显。

Zhang Wei knew he had to help. He **organized** a **fundraising** campaign on **campus**, using his **network**（网络，关系网） of friends and classmates. "We can't just **stand** by and do nothing," he told them. The **response** was **overwhelming**（势不可挡的） — within a week, they had **raised** a significant amount.

张伟知道他必须帮忙。他在校园组织了一场筹款活动，利用自己的朋友和同学关系网。"我们不能袖手旁观，"他告诉他们。反响势不可挡——一周内，他们筹集到了一笔可观的金额。

Li Tao was deeply **moved** by the **support**（支持）. "I don't know how to express my **gratitude**," he said, tears in his eyes. "Your **kindness** has given my family **hope**." Zhang Wei realized that **community** was not just a **concept** — it was something you built through **action**.

李涛被这份支持深深感动了。"我不知道如何表达我的感激，"他眼含泪水说。"你们的善良给了我的家庭希望。"张伟意识到社区不仅仅是一个概念——它是通过行动建立起来的。

This **experience** taught both of them about **compassion** and **solidarity**. They learned that **strength** didn't just come from within — it could come from the people around you, from the **bonds** of friendship and **mutual** obligation. The **crisis** had brought them closer than any **achievement** ever could.

这次经历让他们懂得了同情和团结。他们学到了一力量不仅仅来自内心——还可以来自身边的人，来自友谊的纽带和相互的责任。这场危机让他们比任何成就都更紧密地联系在一起。

## Part 9 — The Transformation

By spring, Zhang Wei had **undergo**（经历） a profound **transformation**. He was no longer the nervous freshman who had arrived on **campus** nine months earlier. His perspective had **altered**（改变） — he now saw university not just as a place to **acquire** knowledge, but as a **laboratory** for life.

到了春天，张伟经历了一次深刻的转变。他不再是九个月前那个紧张的新生。他的视角改变了——他现在把大学不仅仅看作获取知识的地方，而是生活的实验室。

He had learned to **appreciate**（欣赏） the **diversity** of human **experience**. He had discovered that **identity**（身份） was not **fixed** but **evolving**. He had come to understand that **education** was not about **proving** yourself but about **becoming** yourself. His **vocational**（职业的） aspirations had **shifted** from simply finding a **job** to pursuing a **purpose**.

他学会了欣赏人类经验的多样性。他发现身份不是固定而是不断演变的。他逐渐明白教育不是为了证明自己而是为了成为自己。他的职业抱负从简单地找一份工作转变为追求一个目标。

Looking ahead to the summer, Zhang Wei felt **optimistic**（乐观的）. His **internship** would give him **practical** experience in his field. He had **established** a foundation of **skills** and **connections** that would serve him well. But more importantly, he had **grown** as a person.

展望夏天，张伟感到乐观。他的实习将给他提供该领域的实践经验。他已经建立了技能和人脉的基础，这将对他很有帮助。但更重要的是，他作为一个人已经成长了。

He sat on his favorite bench, watching the **sunset** paint the **campus** in shades of **gold** and **pink**. Students were celebrating the end of finals — laughter and music floated on the breeze. Zhang Wei smiled. He had **survived** and **thrived**.

他坐在他最喜欢的长椅上，看着夕阳把校园染成金色和粉色。学生们在庆祝期末考试结束——笑声和音乐随着微风飘荡。张伟笑了。他活了下来，而且茁壮成长了。

"I think I'm ready," he said to himself. "Ready for whatever comes next."

"我想我准备好了，"他对自己说。"准备好迎接接下来的一切。"

### Part 10 — Departure

The last day of the semester arrived. The **campus**（校园） was **bustling** with students **packing** their belongings, saying goodbye to friends, making summer plans. Zhang Wei looked around his **dormitory** room one last time. So many **memories** had been **created** within these walls.

学期最后一天到了。校园里热闹非凡，学生们收拾行李，与朋友告别，制定暑假计划。张伟最后一次环顾宿舍房间。这些墙壁里创造了太多回忆。

He thought back to his first day — the **anxiety**, the **excitement**, the feeling of being a **stranger** in a strange place. He thought about all the **challenges** he had faced and all the **victories** he had celebrated. He thought about the **friendships** that had become his **foundation** and the **mentors** who had guided his path.

他回忆起第一天——焦虑、兴奋、在陌生地方做陌生人的感觉。他想起所有他面对的挑战和庆祝的所有胜利。他想起那些已成为他基础的友谊，那些引导他道路的导师。

Professor Wang found him on the **campus** **lawn**. "You've come a long way," he said. "I **remember** when you could barely **articulate** a question. Now look at you." He handed Zhang Wei a book — a collection of essays on **urban** development. "For your **journey**," he said. "Never stop learning."

王教授在校园草坪上找到了他。"你走了很长一段路，"他说。"我记得你几乎无法清晰表达一个问题。现在看看你。"他递给张伟一本书——一本关于城市发展的论文集。"为你的旅程，"他说。"永远不要停止学习。"

"谢谢你，"他低声对着校园、建筑、树木，对着一切见证了他转变的事物说。然后他转身走向未来，心中携带着所学的一切。
